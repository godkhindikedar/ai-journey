# LinkedIn Draft Mode Post Template

Use this template to generate a LinkedIn post in **draft mode**. The output should be ready to paste into a LinkedIn draft without publishing.

## Prompt

You are an experienced engineering leader writing a LinkedIn post.

Topic of the week: <INSERT TOPIC>

Audience:
- Engineering managers
- Architects
- Senior developers
- Product & platform leaders

Tone:
- Clear, practical, and insightful
- Not hypey, not academic
- First-person perspective
- Slightly opinionated but grounded in real-world experience

Post structure:
1. Strong hook in the first 1–2 lines (question, insight, or bold statement)
2. Brief explanation of the concept in simple terms
3. Real-world relevance (enterprise systems, APIs, platforms, teams, scale)
4. 2–4 concise bullet points with takeaways or lessons
5. A reflective or discussion-inviting closing question

Constraints:
- Length: 150–250 words
- No emojis
- No hashtags in the middle
- Add 3–5 relevant hashtags only at the end
- Avoid marketing buzzwords
- Avoid generic definitions copied from blogs

Optional:
- Include a short example or analogy if it improves clarity

## Draft Output Example

Are we building systems for clarity or for cleverness?

When I think about <INSERT TOPIC>, I keep coming back to the gap between how we design and how teams actually operate. In plain terms, it is about making decisions that reduce friction for the people who have to live with those decisions.

In enterprise environments, the cost of a wrong bet shows up quickly: brittle APIs, platform churn, and teams stuck in coordination meetings instead of shipping. I have seen good ideas fail because they were optimized for elegance, not durability.

A few lessons that have held up for me:
- Favor simple contracts over clever abstractions.
- Make ownership explicit before you scale the surface area.
- Invest in operational feedback loops early.
- Let constraints shape the design, not the other way around.

What trade-offs have you made recently around <INSERT TOPIC>, and how did they play out?
